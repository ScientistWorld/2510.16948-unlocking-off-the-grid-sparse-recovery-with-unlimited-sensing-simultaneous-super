# Direction

Work within the paper's problem family: modulo or unlimited-sensing sparse
super-resolution for filtered off-grid spikes. Improvements should refine
residue recovery, robust spectral fitting, noise handling, initialization, or
extensions of SRes-IterSiS.

Out of scope: replacing the task with a learned direct regressor from samples
to spike times, changing the benchmark to an easier signal family, or using
higher-resolution measurements to bypass the low-bit HDR constraint.
